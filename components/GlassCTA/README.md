# GlassCTA

A liquid-glass call to action, for offers and prices only.

## When to use

Only on offer and price designs, over the sky offer gradient (`hs-offer-sky`) or `assets/Elements/haysam-comet-sky.png`. Never on lessons or news.

## Markup

```html
<div class="hs-offer-sky">
  <span class="hs-glass">Taklifni olish <span aria-hidden="true">→</span></span>
</div>
```

The glass is translucent white with a backdrop blur and a white rim; the label is `night` in JetBrains Mono Bold (44px on a 1080px canvas).

## Do and don't

- Keep the sky gradient for offers; don't bring it into other designs.
- One glass CTA per design.

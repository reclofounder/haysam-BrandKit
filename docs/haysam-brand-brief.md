# Haysam — brand brief for any AI designer

Paste this whole file into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney notes, Figma AI or any design tool, then ask for the design. Every asset it mentions is in the Haysam design system: https://github.com/reclofounder/haysam-BrandKit

## Who we are
Haysam is an Uzbek **AI education platform**: lessons, tutorials, news about AI tools and special offers. Audience: adults who want to work faster with AI. Copy is in **Uzbek (Latin script)**. Tone: friendly expert, short and direct. Every post should read as learning content (a lesson, a "how to", a result), not as tool advertising.

## Logo (do not redraw)
- The only logo is the **mark + caps HAYSAM wordmark** lockup. Use the files, never type or generate it. The old lowercase "haysam" logo is retired.
- Files: `assets/Logos/haysam-logo-*.svg` and the mark alone `assets/Marks/haysam-mark-*.svg`; PNG and JPG exports in `exports/logos/`.
- Which one:
  - `haysam-logo-ink`: light backgrounds (lavender, white). This is the default.
  - `haysam-logo-yellow-ink`: light backgrounds when you want the yellow accent.
  - `haysam-logo-yellow-white`: dark backgrounds (#270601).
  - `haysam-logo-white`: on photos and images.
  - `haysam-logo-black`: one-colour print only.
- Size and place: on a 1080 px wide canvas the lockup is about 52 px tall, centred, near the top (y ≈ 330 on 1080×1920).
- With a tool: `HAYSAM × [official tool logo] Tool name` on one line.
- Clear space around the logo = half the mark's height. Never stretch, rotate, recolour outside the palette, outline, add shadows or put it on a busy area of an image.

Rules:
- Use one accent per design.
- These colours apply to designs without a photo. When a design is built on a picture, the picture sets all the colours, and Haysam shows only through the logo, the tool logo and the font.
- **Never use lime or acid green.**

## Type (all free Google Fonts, in `fonts/`)
Pick one mood font per post and change it from post to post:

| mood | font | use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| brand | JetBrains Mono Bold | Haysam voice, notes, stickers |
| news | Anton | news, loud caps |
| future | Unbounded Black | tech, numbers, "new" |
| loud | Archivo Black | buttons, hype |
| cinema | Big Shoulders Display Black | video, film, process |
| premium | Instrument Serif | personal letters, calm |
| play | Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold | friendly, community |
| ui | Inter | chips and labels only |

- Headlines are big: 110–230 px on a 1080 canvas, at most 2 lines, line height 88–95 %.
- No small meta text.

## Formats
- Reels/Stories cover master: **1080×1920**. Keep everything important inside the band **y 285–1635**, so the 4:5 feed crop (1080×1350) and the 3:4 profile grid still work.
- Keep text out of Reels UI zones: the right 140 px below y 1180, and the bottom 330 px.
- Feed post and carousel slide: **1080×1350**.
- Carousel slides always sit on the lavender gradient with the dot grid. Each slide has one idea with a large headline, a real tool-logo tile when a tool is named, and one object.

## Backgrounds (in `assets/Backgrounds/` and `assets/Elements/`)
- Lavender gradient, either plain or with one pattern: Dots, Lines or Tiles.
- Dark #270601 with the warm comet glow.
- A full-bleed AI image. Image covers are always full-bleed, never a flat colour block on top.
- The comet glow has a direction: a crisp yellow head and a soft orange trail. Aim the head at the hero.

## Signature details (use 1–2 per design, never all)
- Comet glow aimed at the hero.
- A Figma-style selection box with a named cursor ("haysam").
- A mono sticker tilted −4°.
- A big pop button with a rim and glow in the same hue, never a dark outline.
- A liquid-glass CTA, for offers only.
- Gradient lettering with a thin top sheen, never bevels or WordArt.

## Images and logos of other brands
- Every tool or brand named in the text gets its **real official logo**. Sources: Simple Icons, the brand's press kit or site, Wikimedia Commons. Never generate or redraw a logo. If there is no official file, write the name instead.
- 3D objects:
  - glassmorphic, high detail, adult (not toy or cartoon)
  - one hue family taken from the topic, not forced into brand yellow
- An object placed on the brand background is cut out cleanly, with no halo, leftover backdrop or dirty shadow.

## Never
- Page counters such as "02 / 07".
- Decorative emoji, filler labels or fake UI captions.
- Generated or lowercase Haysam logos.
- Lime or acid green.
- Bevel or WordArt effects.
- More than one accent colour.
- Text inside Reels UI zones.
- Placeholder text.

## Before you hand it over
1. Is the logo an official file, with the right variant for the background?
2. Is the main content inside y 285–1635?
3. Is there one mood font and one accent?
4. Are the tool logos real?
5. Is it free of AI slop: no page counters, filler or emoji?
6. Is the copy in Uzbek Latin, short, and does it read as a lesson or a benefit?
